This is a completely fictional agreement between Example Party A and Example Party B created solely to demonstrate textual dependency checking.

Section 2. Advance Payment
Under Section 4 above, Example Party A must issue the payment notice before funds are transferred. This demonstration intentionally puts the referenced payment-notice section later in the document, so the directional word contradicts its position.

Section 5. Confidentiality
