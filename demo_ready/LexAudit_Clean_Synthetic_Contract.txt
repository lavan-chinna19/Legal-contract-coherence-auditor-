Section 1. Definitions
For this fictional demonstration, "Services" means document preparation, drafting support, and training assistance. The parties are Example Client and Example Provider; neither is a real business.

Section 2. Scope of Services
Example Provider shall provide the Services described in Section 1. The parties may agree on detailed milestones in writing.

Section 3. Payment
Example Client shall pay Example Provider within thirty calendar days after receipt of a correctly issued invoice.

Section 4. Confidentiality
Both fictional parties shall protect confidential information used during the Services. Information that is already public is excluded from this clause.

Section 5. Notices
All notices under this fictional agreement must be in writing and delivered to the contact address separately provided by each party.

Section 6. Termination
Either fictional party may terminate this illustrative agreement upon thirty days' written notice. This is a synthetic classroom document only.
