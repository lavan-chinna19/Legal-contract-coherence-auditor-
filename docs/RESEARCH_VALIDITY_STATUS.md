# Research validity status

Application functionality and scientific validity are separate. No model weights, historical benchmark predictions, experimental labels or production anomaly thresholds were intentionally changed by this release. No large model retraining or new confirmatory evaluation was performed.

Phase 4D-C-A's controlling interpretation is **EXPLORATORY BENCHMARK ONLY — PREREGISTRATION COMPROMISED**. Its audit identified invalid order transformations and insufficient positive evidence. Historical development metrics displayed in Transparency are labelled historical and exploratory; they are not current-document metrics or legal accuracy estimates.

Phase 4D-D-A identifies nine computationally supported directional candidates and fifteen eligible negatives. These development-only cases do not demonstrate independent model generalization or expert legal verification. Existing audit narratives use stronger “verified” terminology; this release does not adopt that as independent adjudication.

The opt-in dependency graph is an independent deterministic channel. It parses explicit numeric section identities and above/below/immediate-predecessor relationships, supplies source and target evidence, and abstains on unresolved/duplicate targets and vague boundary antecedents. Ordinary numbered references remain nondirectional; noncanonical arrangements alone are not defects. Self-contained antecedents move with their clause. Its scores are never merged into Channel A, Channel B or the ensemble.

Graph `VERIFIED_TEXTUAL_CONFLICT` means a narrowly checked textual positional contradiction. It does not establish invalidity or legal effect. `REVIEW_REQUIRED` means evidence was ambiguous or insufficient. `NO_FINDINGS` means no configured textual check raised a finding, not proof that no defect exists. Roman numerals, informal references and legal interpretation remain outside this conservative parser's supported scope.

Channel A distances, Channel B transition outputs and the existing ensemble are exploratory anomaly signals. They are not calibrated probabilities of legal defects. Synthetic-shuffle conformal intervals do not certify legal correctness or unseen commercial-contract coverage. Rule flags are automated candidates for review. Counterfactual scores suggest alternative positions without applying edits or asserting improvement in legal meaning.

External validation blockers remain:

1. Independent qualified human annotation and adjudication.
2. Newly acquired unseen-family evaluation with protocol frozen before exposure.
3. Independent security assessment and real deployment acceptance.

No production-grade legal correctness or independent accuracy claim is made.
