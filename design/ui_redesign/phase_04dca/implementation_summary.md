## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 4D-C-A was executed under strict non-destructive preservation constraints to resolve critical scientific evidence anomalies identified in the Phase 4D-C legal contract structural coherence benchmark:
1. **Model Checkpoint SHA-256 Hashes**: Reconciled historical documentation discrepancies with physical file byte hashes across repository commits and verified that model weights and centroid representations have remained **100% byte-identical** since initial commit.
2. **Evaluation Chronology & Pre-Registration**: Reconstructed the end-to-end execution timeline, demonstrating that while models ran with frozen baseline configurations, the protocol file was co-generated by the reporting engine after test predictions were calculated. Formally downgraded the study to an **exploratory source-disjoint pilot**.
3. **Parent Agreement Denominator & SEC Inventory**: Resolved the narrative discrepancy (19 + 7 + 5 = 31 vs 32) as a reporting typo, mathematically verifying the authoritative split allocation: **18 Development (9 families) + 9 Validation (3 families) + 5 Sealed Test (3 families) = 32 Parent Agreements (15 Families)**. Dissected `edgar_manifest.json` confirming 10 metadata keys (not filings) containing 50 exhibit entries across 19 accessions and 19 CIKs, with 50 downloaded files and 32 eligible parents.
4. **SEC EDGAR Copyright & Usage Rights**: Corrected the erroneous assertion that SEC exhibits are public domain works under 17 U.S.C. §105. Reclassified the corpus as **`PUBLICLY_ACCESSIBLE_RIGHTS_UNDETERMINED`**, permitted under fair use for computational analysis, while prohibiting full-text redistribution.
5. **Ground-Truth Label Validity & Sensitivity**: Audited all 28 positive benchmark cases, discovering that target backward discourse markers (e.g. *"Notwithstanding the foregoing definition"*) were self-contained within multi-sentence clauses, meaning Category P1 clause swaps did not break discourse dependencies. Provided post-hoc sensitivity analyses showing precision drops to $\le 9.1\%$ or becomes `NOT_ESTIMABLE`. In View 2 (0 confirmed positives), strict positive recall is `NOT_ESTIMABLE`.
6. **Candidate C2 & Regression Reconciliation**: Reconciled Candidate C2 metrics between raw stored thresholded predictions ($TP=2, FP=16, FN=0, TN=0$) and historical reported underfitting ($TP=0, FP=0, FN=2, TN=16$). Reconciled Phase 4D-C full backend regression to 193 passed (185 to 195 collected) from the raw pytest evidence log.
