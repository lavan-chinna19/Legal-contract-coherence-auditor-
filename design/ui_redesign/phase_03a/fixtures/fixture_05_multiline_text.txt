1. Deliverables and Specifications.
   The Vendor shall provide the following operational components:
     (a) Database migration scripts;
     (b) API documentation and OpenAPI schemas;
     (c) Security penetration test reports.

   All deliverables must receive written acceptance before invoicing.

2. Acceptance Criteria and Validation.
   The Customer shall have ten business days to review:
     - Functional compliance;
     - Latency benchmarks under peak load.

   Failure to reject within ten business days constitutes formal acceptance.
